Make the audit decision

Which step would best establish what happened?

Document the finding and follow up

Confirmed in the reviewed record. REP-260004 was recorded as transmitted at 18:00. Technical review was recorded at 17:05. Both required release approval fields were blank.

Still to verify. Request the report’s approval and transmission events from the LIMS audit trail. Verify the approver’s identity, authorization, independence from the technical review, and timestamp. Preserve the original record and log what the trail establishes.

Decide from the evidence. If a valid approval preceded transmission, investigate why it is absent from the result record. If the available audit evidence shows late approval, no approval, or an unauthorized approver, escalate the release exception for impact assessment under the lab’s procedure. If the audit trail is incomplete, document the evidence gap and keep the approval status open.
